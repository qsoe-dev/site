QRV (historical)

A QNX port to RISC-V.

QRV ported QNX 6.4.1 to 64-bit RISC-V and has since concluded. The hardware bring-up lessons it produced fed directly into QSOE/N.

See the development blog at r-tty.blogspot.com. Repository: github.com/r-tty/qrv.
